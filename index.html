Chapter II

The Virat Kohli Story

18

Born in Delhi on 5 November 1988, Virat Kohli grew up chasing a single idea: to play for India. Delhi's age-group cricket sharpened his hunger, and in 2008 he captained India to the Under-19 World Cup title in Malaysia.

Weeks later he made his ODI debut. What followed was a transformation — from an aggressive young talent into one of the most complete run-scorers the game has seen, famed for chasing, for fitness and for sheer intensity.

Fitness became his identity, and Indian cricket's fitness standards rose with him.— Editorial analysis, not a quotation

    The Legacy

    Fact Kohli finished his Test career with 9,230 runs, and holds the men's ODI record for centuries.

    Analysis He made intensity and athletic conditioning a template for younger Indian cricketers.

    Analysis His chase-mastery changed how teams think about targets, and his Test captaincy built a pace-led, win-abroad identity.

    The numbers will keep changing. The standard he set won't.
